About the institute

Building government careers in Bharatpur since 2014

Established in 2014, Shikhar Classes was founded with a clear vision: to make quality competitive exam preparation accessible to students from Bharatpur, nearby towns, and rural areas. We started with a single classroom and a commitment to providing the right guidance, experienced teaching support, and a disciplined learning environment that helps students prepare confidently for government recruitment examinations.

Over the years, shikhar classeshas grown into a dedicated coaching institute with two branches in Bharatpur, helping students prepare for a wide range of competitive examinations, including SSC, Banking, Railway, Police recruitment, and High Court examinations. Our objective is not just to complete the syllabus but to develop the knowledge, problem-solving ability, accuracy, and confidence required to perform well in competitive examinations.

We follow a structured and result-oriented teaching approach. Every course is organised around a planned syllabus, regular classroom sessions, topic-wise practice, and weekly mock tests. Students are encouraged to strengthen their fundamentals before moving towards advanced questions and examination-level problem solving. Regular assessments help identify weak areas and track individual progress throughout the preparation journey.

At Shikhar Classes, we understand that every student has a different learning pace and academic background. That's why we focus on individual attention, doubt-clearing sessions, revision support, and performance-based guidance. Our faculty members work to make difficult concepts easier to understand while helping students improve their speed, accuracy, time management, and examination strategies.

Beyond classroom teaching, we aim to create a motivating and disciplined atmosphere where students can stay consistent, overcome academic challenges, and work towards their career goals. Whether you are beginning your competitive exam preparation or looking to improve your existing performance, shikhar classesCoaching Institute provides a supportive learning environment to help you take the next step towards a government career.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Which exams does Shikhar Classes prepare students for?

We run full programmes for SSC (CGL, CHSL, MTS, GD), Banking (IBPS, SBI, RBI), Railway (NTPC, Group D, ALP), Rajasthan and Delhi Police, and High Court and LDC recruitment exams.

Can I attend a free demo class before enrolling?

Yes. A free demo class is held every Saturday at 11:00 AM at both branches. Book your seat through the form on this page or by calling or messaging us on WhatsApp.

What are the course fees and can I pay in instalments?

Fees range from ₹12,000 to ₹24,000 depending on the course. Instalment plans are available, and the scholarship test can reduce fees by up to 50 percent for deserving students.

Are classes offline or online?

Most courses run in classrooms at our Bharatpur branches. SSC, Banking, Railway and LDC students also get access to recorded lectures and online mock tests, so missed classes are never lost.

Will I get study material and mock tests?

Yes. Printed notes, daily practice sets, previous year papers and a weekly mock test are included in the course fee. Banking and Railway students also practise on computer-based tests in our lab.

How do I enrol?

Visit either branch with your photo ID and recent photograph, or fill the enquiry form here and our team will call you within 24 hours to confirm your batch.
